Receipts

Every fixture’s receipt. Read what Tracefix hands back.

The regression fixtures are 11 small buggy repos with a known answer: a bug, a fix that is right or wrong in a known way, and the level its receipt must reach. Each page below shows the issue, the fix, the receipt as it reads in a PR comment, the tests Tracefix wrote and the run in the terminal.

11 of 11 receipts meet their known answer; 7 come with a counterexample.

Made by the real pipeline on the real fixture repos, with the test suite’s recorded model answers, so a run costs nothing and comes out the same each time. A receipt that misses its known answer fails the test suite. The fixtures on GitHub.
